How I work

From a cold list to a closed client.

1. Prospect

Find

CEOs with a healthy online presence.

Check tenure

How long they've been in the role.

Gauge openness

Do they own the business? Owners are the clearest signal.

Confirm fit

Employee size and ICP match: $25M–$500M companies.

Load

Export the list and add each CEO to a cadence.

In parallel, I work the CRM for low-hanging fruit and re-engagements.

2. Get the meeting

Open

Circle back to earlier outreach and ask for 10 minutes. Once they say yes, remind them what it was about, then let the script guide the conversation.

Follow up

About 100 emails a week, each prospect dripped across 4 emails. If they don't answer, about 9 touchpoints across calls, email and LinkedIn.

Handle objections

No interest, already have a coach, not a good time, retiring soon. I wrote the script and rebuttals the whole team uses.

3. From meeting to close

Book the meeting

Discovery on their 6-month goals, 3-year goals, and what would make a good coach.

Recommend coaches

Match on industry, goals and succession planning from a roster of 90.

Set the sessions

Two complimentary one-hour coaching sessions.

Sessions held

The CEO meets the coaches and experiences the coaching firsthand.

Feedback and close

Gather their feedback, then run the closing call.
